# Home

DISPATCH is a show and installation control engine built in TouchDesigner. It plays scenes, any TouchDesigner network, from a paged grid of tiles, sends their outputs to screens, fixtures and lasers, and sends cues to other systems over OSC, UDP, MIDI and more. A timeline runs cues and animates parameters over the length of a show.

DISPATCH works alongside [AAVJ](https://anti-alias-arts.github.io/AAVJ-Docs/): AAVJ is the layered video mixer, DISPATCH runs everything around it.
