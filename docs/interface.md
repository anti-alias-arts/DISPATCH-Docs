### Grouped and Ungrouped Tiles

- **Grouped** tiles work like radio buttons: one scene per group is live, and pressing another tile in the group transitions to it.
- **Ungrouped** tiles are toggles: on means the scene runs. Use these for scenes that output by themselves or send messages.
